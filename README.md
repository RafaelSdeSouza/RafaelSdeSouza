# Dr. Rafael S. de Souza

Astrostatistician and scientific software developer working on Bayesian reasoning, machine learning, astronomical surveys, galaxy evolution, and applied mathematics.

[Website](https://rafaelsdesouza.github.io/) | [ADS Publications](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/public-libraries/I0n5jUUURgCy9JoMqkqhng) | [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7207-4584) | [COIN](https://cosmostatistics-initiative.org/) | [University Profile](https://researchprofiles.herts.ac.uk/en/persons/rafael-s-de-souza) | [Email](mailto:rd23aag@herts.ac.uk)

## Current roles

- Senior Lecturer in Astrophysics and Astrostatistics, University of Hertfordshire.
- Founder and Co-Chair, Cosmostatistics Initiative (COIN).
- Chair, Astrostatistics Interest Group of the International Statistical Institute.
- Visiting Scholar, Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul.
- Adjunct Associate Professor, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

## Selected software

- [`capivara`](https://github.com/RafaelSdeSouza/capivara) - spectral-based segmentation for IFU data cubes, [MNRAS 539(4), 3166 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staf688).
- [`SAGUI`](https://cosmostatistics-initiative.org/) - SED-based segmentation of multi-band galaxy images.
- [`SpectralUnmix`](https://github.com/RafaelSdeSouza/SpectralUnmix) - regularized non-negative matrix factorization for spectral data.
- [`PowerSpectR`](https://github.com/RafaelSdeSouza/PowerSpectR) - radial power-spectrum estimation for imaging data.
- [`GalMOSS`](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024ascl.soft04005M) - GPU-accelerated galaxy surface-brightness fitting.
- [`qrpca`](https://github.com/RafaelSdeSouza/qrpca) - QR-based PCA, [A&C 41, 100633 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ascom.2022.100633).
